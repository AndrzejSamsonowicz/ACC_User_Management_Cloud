Connect your Autodesk account

The app works inside your Forma hubs, so it signs in with your Autodesk account. A Forma account admin allows the app once per account.

  1. 1

    Allow the app in your Forma account

    In Forma Account Admin, open Settings, then Custom Integrations, and add a custom integration with this client ID. Skip this if your account admin already did it.

    Sign in with Autodesk

    Autodesk's sign-in page opens, then brings you straight back to your projects.
