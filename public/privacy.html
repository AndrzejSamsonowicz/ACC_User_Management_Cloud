Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Digibuild ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information through Forma User Manager (the "Service"), a tool that connects to your Autodesk Forma account to help you manage users, roles, companies, and folder permissions across your Autodesk Forma hubs and projects.

Information we collect

  • Account information. The email address you register with, and your subscription/trial status. Your password is managed entirely by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it in plain text.
  • Autodesk Forma data. Once you connect your Autodesk account, we access and temporarily cache the data needed to power the Service's features on your behalf: hub, project, user, role, and company lists, and folder-permission structures. We do not access, copy, or store your actual project files, drawings, or models.
  • Authentication tokens. Your Autodesk access token exists only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session and is never stored on our servers. Your Autodesk refresh token (which lets the Service keep your connection alive without requiring you to sign in again every hour) is stored on our servers encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and is never sent to or exposed in your browser.
  • Basic usage data. Login timestamps and event logs, used only for security monitoring and troubleshooting.

How we use this information

We use the information above solely to operate the Service: authenticating you, displaying and managing your Autodesk Forma users/roles/companies/folder permissions as you direct, and administering your subscription or trial.

Third parties we share data with

  • Google Cloud Platform / Firebase. Provides the hosting, authentication, and database infrastructure the Service runs on (data stored in Firestore, in the europe-west6 region, Zurich, Switzerland).
  • Autodesk. The platform your account connects to. Your use of Autodesk's own services remains governed by Autodesk's privacy policy.
  • PayPal. Processes subscription payments. We never see or store your payment card details.
  • Our email provider. Sends transactional emails only (account verification, password reset, license delivery).

Each of these providers is required, contractually or technically, to protect your data to a standard consistent with this policy.

Data retention

We retain your account and synced data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account or your subscription ends, we delete your data within 30 days.

Your rights and how to revoke access

  • You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us at digibuild@digibuild.ch.
  • You can revoke the Service's access to your Autodesk Forma data at any time, independent of contacting us, by removing it from your Autodesk Forma hub's "Custom Integrations" settings. This immediately stops the Service from accessing your Autodesk data.

Data security

Stored credentials and tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM), and all traffic to the Service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Further detail on our security practices is available on request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to digibuild@digibuild.ch.

The rules for using the Service are in the Terms of Service.
